3-day virtual events, in brief

The format, the work and the money, stated plainly. Everything below is covered at length in the video and in the transcript.

01

The format

What a 3-day virtual event is
A live launch format run across three consecutive days, where the host teaches their most valuable material, answers questions live, and sells during the event. It sells to an existing email audience rather than to cold traffic.
Who it works for
Course creators, coaches, educators and community builders who already have an active email audience and a paid offer. It concentrates an existing audience relationship. It cannot recreate one that has gone cold.
Who it does not work for
It fails when the list is no longer active, when the wrong topic or offer is chosen, or when the owner is unwilling to raise email frequency during the launch window. List size alone does not predict whether an event will work.
02

The work

What AfterHarvest does
Plans, builds and runs the whole event: offer strategy, funnels, registration pages, the VSL and marketing assets, over 100 launch emails, every slide, and the live operation across the three days.
What you do
Roughly 15 to 20 hours across about six weeks. You approve work, review it, record what is needed and teach live. AfterHarvest does the building and strategy.
How often it can be run
Quarterly, up to four times a year, without burning out the audience. The first event takes the most work because the engine is being built. Later events reuse it.
03

The money

What it costs
There is $0 upfront. AfterHarvest is paid a revenue share of what the launch collects, 20% for lists above 25,000 subscribers and 25% below that. An activation fee is charged in week two and credited against that revenue share.
The revenue floor
Before signing, AfterHarvest models the list, the offer and the numbers, then puts a revenue figure in writing. The guaranteed floor is half that figure. If the launch comes in under it, no revenue share is owed and the activation fee is refunded.
The two-week walk-away
The funnel is built within 14 days. If you do not want to continue at that point, you can walk away with no strings attached. This is written into the contract.
